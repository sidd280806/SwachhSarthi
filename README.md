# 🌍 **SwacchSarthi – Smart Waste Management System**

An AI-powered smart waste management platform that helps cities **predict garbage overflow**, **optimize waste collection routes**, and **empower citizens** to raise complaints directly to their area driver — building cleaner, smarter, and more efficient cities.

---

## 🚀 **Live MVP**
🔗 **MVP Link:** https://swachhsarthi-sidd.vercel.app/
---

## 🧠 **Problem Statement**
Cities struggle with:
- Overflowing garbage bins  
- Delayed waste collection  
- Lack of predictive planning  
- Weak citizen complaint handling  

This results in pollution, health issues, dissatisfaction, and inefficient municipal operations.

---

## ✅ **Our Solution**
**SwacchSarthi** provides:
✔ Smart Municipal Dashboard  
✔ Risk Zone Prediction  
✔ Route Optimization  
✔ AI Chatbot Support  
✔ Direct Citizen → Driver Complaint System  
✔ Real-time Reports & Analytics  

A complete waste management ecosystem in one platform.
